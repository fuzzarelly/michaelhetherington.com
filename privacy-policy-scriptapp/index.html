Privacy Policy for Script App

My Tapping Script

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

My Tapping Script ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App.

a) Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

• Email address

• Account ID

• Subscription status

• Basic profile information (if provided)

b) App Usage Information

We may collect:

• EFT script inputs (e.g. topics, emotions, preferences)

• Generated scripts saved to your account

• App interaction data (feature usage, session activity)

This information is used only to provide and improve the service.

c) Payment Information

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider (e.g. Stripe).

• We do not store your credit card details

• We only store confirmation of payment status and subscription plan

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

• Create and manage your account

• Provide personalized EFT scripts

• Manage subscriptions and billing

• Improve app performance and reliability

• Communicate important account or service updates

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. No Selling or Sharing of Data

We take a strict approach to user privacy.

• We do not sell your personal information

• We do not share your personal data with third parties

• We do not allow third parties to use your data for marketing

Your information stays private and is used only to operate the App.

5. Data Storage & Security

• User data is stored securely using industry-standard systems

• We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data

• Access to user data is restricted and monitored

While no system can guarantee 100% security, we make every reasonable effort to protect your information.

6. User Control & Access

You may:

• View and update your account information

• Cancel your subscription at any time via the Profile section

• Request deletion of your account and associated data

To request data access or deletion, please contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as:

• Your account remains active, or

• It is required to provide the service, or

• We are legally required to retain it

When accounts are deleted, associated data is removed within a reasonable timeframe, unless required by law.

8. Cookies & Tracking

The App may use essential cookies or similar technologies to:

• Maintain login sessions

• Ensure basic functionality

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. International Users

If you access the App from outside your country of residence, you understand that your data may be processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

• Updates will be reflected in the App

• Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy
