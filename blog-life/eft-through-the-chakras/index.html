By Michael Hetherington · February 21, 2025

Our emotions and energy flow through an intricate system in the body, one that ancient traditions have mapped out for thousands of years. The chakras—seven major energy centers along the spine—play a crucial role in our emotional, physical, and spiritual well-being.

EFT tapping is a simple yet profoundly effective method that involves tapping on specific acupuncture points while focusing our attention and voicing powerful phrases that help to activate specific states.

Doing so allows us to move and process any stuck emotions, rewire subconscious programming, and bring ourselves back into a state of harmony and balance. Why Work with EFT Through the Chakras?

Traditional EFT works by tapping on meridian points—energy channels recognized in Chinese medicine. The chakras, a concept rooted in yogic traditions, function as major energetic hubs along the body. By integrating these two systems, EFT Through the Chakras provides a multi-layered approach to healing that works on:

  • The Physical Body – Addressing pain, muscle tension, and overall vitality.

  • The Emotional Body – Releasing trapped emotions, stress, and limiting beliefs.

  • The Spiritual Body – Aligning with higher consciousness and inner wisdom.

The Seven Chakras & How EFT Supports Them

Each chakra governs different physical, emotional, and mental aspects of our being. By using EFT specifically for each chakra, we can dissolve energy blockages and restore harmony.

1. Root Chakra (Muladhara) – Grounding & Security

Located at the base of the spine, the root chakra is linked to survival, stability, and feeling safe in the world. When imbalanced, it can lead to fear, anxiety, and a lack of security.

EFT Focus: Tapping through feelings of fear, insecurity, and instability while affirming safety and stability in life.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I feel unsafe and uncertain, I deeply and completely accept myself.”

2. Sacral Chakra (Svadhisthana) – Creativity & Emotion

Located just below the navel, this chakra governs emotions, creativity, and relationships. Imbalances can manifest as emotional instability, creative blocks, or relationship struggles.

EFT Focus: Releasing shame, guilt, and emotional blockages while affirming creative flow and joy.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I feel blocked in expressing my creativity, I choose to embrace my unique gifts.”

3. Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura) – Confidence & Personal Power

Situated above the navel, this chakra is responsible for self-esteem, confidence, and personal power. When imbalanced, it can result in self-doubt, procrastination, and digestive issues.

EFT Focus: Dissolving self-doubt and fear of failure while reinforcing self-worth and empowerment.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I doubt myself and feel powerless, I choose to stand in my confidence and strength.”

4. Heart Chakra (Anahata) – Love & Compassion

Located in the center of the chest, the heart chakra is the bridge between the physical and spiritual realms, governing love, compassion, and connection.

EFT Focus: Releasing heartbreak, grief, and resentment while embracing love and forgiveness.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I feel hurt and closed off, I open my heart to love and healing.”

5. Throat Chakra (Vishuddha) – Communication & Truth

This chakra, located at the throat, governs self-expression and speaking one’s truth. Imbalances can manifest as fear of speaking up, throat issues, or a tendency to suppress emotions.

EFT Focus: Releasing fear of self-expression while affirming confidence in one’s voice and truth.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I struggle to express myself, I allow my voice to be heard with confidence.”

6. Third Eye Chakra (Ajna) – Intuition & Inner Wisdom

Positioned between the eyebrows, the third eye chakra is the center of intuition, insight, and higher perception. Blockages can lead to confusion, indecisiveness, and lack of clarity.

EFT Focus: Clearing mental fog, self-doubt, and skepticism while enhancing intuitive abilities.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I doubt my intuition, I trust and embrace my inner wisdom.”

7. Crown Chakra (Sahasrara) – Spiritual Connection & Higher Consciousness

The crown chakra, located at the top of the head, represents our connection to the universe, spiritual insight, and higher purpose. Imbalances may manifest as disconnection, lack of purpose, or existential doubt.

EFT Focus: Releasing spiritual doubt and fostering a deeper sense of connection and trust in life’s flow.

Example Setup Statement: “Even though I feel disconnected and lost, I trust that I am divinely guided and supported.”

How to Use EFT Through the Chakras in Your Daily Life

  1. Identify a Challenge: Notice an emotion, belief, or physical symptom that is bothering you.

  2. Determine the Chakra: Consider which chakra might be linked to the issue.

  3. Use EFT Tapping: Follow the EFT process—tap on key points while voicing the emotions and reframing the experience.

  4. Observe Changes: Pay attention to shifts in emotions, physical sensations, and thoughts after tapping.
