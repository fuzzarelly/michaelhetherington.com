Why write your own scripts?

Generic tapping scripts can take the edge off, but they rarely reach the specific memories, beliefs and body sensations that keep a pattern running. The words that create real change are the ones that match your experience, or your client's, as closely as possible.

In this class, Michael walks you through a simple, repeatable process for turning what someone is feeling into a clear, targeted tapping sequence, from the first setup statement through to the reminder phrases and the positive rounds that follow. You'll leave able to write a script for almost any issue, and to adjust it in the moment as the session unfolds.
