The Chinese medicine angle

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, the Heart houses the Shen — the mind-spirit — and is considered the seat of calm. When the Heart is unsettled, the result is exactly what anxiety feels like: restlessness, a racing mind, and a body that won't settle even when nothing is actually wrong right now.

This class brings that Chinese medicine lens together with EFT tapping — giving you both the practical tools to calm your nervous system in the moment, and the understanding of why anxiety keeps rebuilding itself so you can work with the root instead of only the symptom.
