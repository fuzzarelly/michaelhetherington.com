Delete Your Script App Data

Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your account and associated data from the EFT App, My Tapping Script, by Michael Hetherington, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, please email:

📧 support@michaelhetherington.com

Include the following details:

  • The email address used to create your account

  • A brief request stating that you would like your account deleted

What Happens Next

Once we receive your request:

  • Your account will be permanently deleted

  • All associated personal data will be removed from our systems

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, we will remove:

  • Your account information (name, email address)

  • Login credentials

  • Any saved preferences or user data within the app

Data Retention

  • We do not retain your personal data after deletion

  • In some cases, minimal data may be temporarily retained for legal, security, or operational purposes, and will be deleted within 30–90 days

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

  • You will lose access to any content or services associated with your account

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact:

📧 info@michaelhetherington.com
