The research

Is EFT tapping actually real?

If you’re sceptical, that’s fine. I was too when I first discovered it. EFT is not new-age guesswork. It has been tested in dozens of randomised controlled trials, and the results consistently show real reductions in stress, anxiety, depression and PTSD.

It works by gently tapping on acupressure points while you speak about what’s bothering you. That combination calms your nervous system and helps release the emotional charge around the issue.

56randomised controlled trials
8meta-analyses
41%average drop in depression symptoms
